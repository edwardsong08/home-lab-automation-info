Home Lab n8n Logs

Edward Song’s personal, self-hosted home-lab automation. It records daily service checks and incident outcomes in annual Google Sheets workbooks in the owner’s Google Drive. This is not a public signup service.

Privacy policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026.

Access and purpose

The owner authorizes Google Drive metadata access, access to Drive files created or selected for this app, and Google Sheets access. The Sheets permission is broader than one workbook; configured workflows use the designated status-log folder and annual workbooks. This configuration is not a Google-enforced folder-only permission boundary.

Drive access finds and organizes the annual workbooks. Sheets access creates tabs and headers, appends daily and incident records, and formats status rows. Records include times, service names and status, incident/recovery outcomes, and operational identifiers. This integration is not configured to read Gmail messages or Google Docs.

Storage and sharing

Workbooks are stored in the owner’s Google Drive. OAuth credentials are stored in n8n’s encrypted credential storage on the owner’s home-lab server. Execution history may retain operational inputs and outputs according to the instance’s retention settings. Authorized administrators may access this operational data.

Google processes data needed to provide Drive, Sheets, and email services. Operational notifications go to the owner’s email using a separately configured SMTP credential. This automation does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train generalized AI models. Access is for the owner’s monitoring workflows and authorized administration.

Retention and revocation

Workbooks remain until the owner deletes them. Execution history follows the configured n8n retention policy. The owner can revoke authorization in Google Account third-party connections and remove the credential from n8n. Revocation stops future access but does not delete existing workbooks or execution records; delete those separately if desired.

Personal-use terms

This app is intended only for the owner’s personal home-lab administration. It is not offered as a public monitoring service and carries no availability or recovery guarantee. Automated recovery is limited by configured safeguards and does not replace independent monitoring, backups, or manual investigation.
